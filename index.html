Big monitoring stacks are overkill for small teams.

Campus projects, small teams and MSMEs run live apps with no view of their traffic. Outages and surges usually surface when someone complains.

Grafana with Prometheus is powerful, but it is heavy to host and tune. Flux does the few things a small team needs and nothing else.

From request to alert in four steps.

1

flux-agent

Express middleware inside your app counts every request.

2

Ingestion API

Receives counts over per-site API keys.

3

MongoDB Atlas

Stores metrics, sites and alerts.

4

React dashboard

Charts traffic live with Vite and Recharts.

Alert worker: checks traffic against each site's threshold on a schedule and waits 5 minutes before alerting again.

Everything on one screen.

Start reporting with one line.

app.use(fluxAgent({ apiKey: process.env.FLUX_KEY }));

Add the agent to your Express app and your traffic shows up in Flux.
